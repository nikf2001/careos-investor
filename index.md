# CareOS — Investor Materials

**Healthcare Operations OS for Norwegian Municipal Care Facilities**

Pre-Seed | March 2026 | Confidential

---

## The Opportunity

Norway's 80+ population will double by 2040. The healthcare workforce cannot grow to match. Today's care facilities run on fragmented tools that don't talk to each other — creating reactive operations, preventable adverse events, and unsustainable staffing costs.

**CareOS is the operating system that connects them.** A horizontal data platform, workflow engine, and AI decision support layer with pluggable vertical connectors — vendor-neutral by design.
